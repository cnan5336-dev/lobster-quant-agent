# Research-only disclaimer

Lobster Quant Agent is software for market research, historical simulation, and alerts. It is not a broker, trading system, fiduciary, investment adviser, or source of personalized investment advice.

Market data may be delayed, incomplete, adjusted, unavailable, or wrong. Backtests are hypothetical and are sensitive to data quality, look-ahead assumptions, fees, slippage, liquidity, corporate actions, and execution modeling. Historical results do not predict future performance.

The project has no broker integration and cannot place, modify, or cancel orders. Users are solely responsible for verifying data and making their own decisions.
